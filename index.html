The problem

Bangladesh has
a lot of waste.

0

Tonnes generated every single day

  • Most of it is never recycled properly.It is burned, buried or left in open landfills at the edge of our cities.
  • It poisons the ground we live on.Waste leaks into our rivers, our soil and the air over Dhaka.
  • It costs us more than money.It harms our health, our environment and the future our generation inherits.

But what if this waste could become our wealth?

Our solution

What we do

Five steps
from bin to
beautiful.

Nothing skips a step. That is how a discarded thing ends up worth keeping.

  1. Step one

    We collect

    We collect waste from cities, industries and communities, responsibly and on a schedule people can rely on.

  2. Step two

    We sort

    Every load is separated by hand and by material, because good recycling starts with careful sorting.

  3. Step three

    We process

    Materials are cleaned, treated and prepared so they are safe, durable and ready for a second life.

  4. Step four

    We create

    Our makers turn processed waste into bags, stationery, packaging and home pieces that stand on their own as good design.

  5. Step five

    We share impact

    Every product carries a QR code. Scan it and you see exactly what it was made from and what your purchase changed.
